Accuracy

Positions are Keplerian approximations from JPL planetary elements, valid from 3000 BCE to 3000 CE; when served by the backend, exact JPL DE441 positions replace them. Dates are proleptic Gregorian, in UT; year 0 is 1 BCE. Axial tilts are approximate (tilted about the scene X axis) and radial distances are compressed unless true scale is on. The Moon is drawn at a distance relative to Earth's size (60 Earth radii in true-size mode), not to the AU scale, which would put it inside the globe.
